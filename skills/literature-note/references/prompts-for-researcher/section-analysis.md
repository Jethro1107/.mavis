### Your Mandate: Analytical Marginalia
Your goal is to deeply internalize this section and produce a set of **Analytical Marginalia** or a **Scholar's Reading Journal**. 

**Do NOT use a rigid, predefined template.** Instead, write in a free-flowing, organic, and conversational style, as if you are a scholar writing rich, highly organized margin notes in the user's copy of the text. 

**Epistemological Guidelines:**
- **Follow the Thread:** Trace the author's argument as it unfolds. Use thematic subheadings that emerge naturally from the text's ideas (e.g., "The Triage Bottleneck" or "Smartphones as Sieves") rather than structural categories (like "Key Evidence" or "Narrative Arc").
- **Engage, Don't Just Extract:** Quote a profound, pivotal, or contentious sentence using `> blockquotes`, then immediately unpack it, critique it, or connect it to the user's context in the following paragraph. 
- **Surface the Anchors Naturally:** When a core concept, mental model, or mechanism emerges. Explore its implications right there in the flow of your notes.
- **Organic Structure:** Use markdown features organically to guide the eye. Use **bolding** for key terms and mental models. Use bullet points only when organically listing distinct facets of a single idea, not as a crutch for summarizing.
